# Resetting your password

1. On the sign-in page, choose "Forgot password".
2. Enter the email address of your account. We send a reset link that works for 30 minutes.
3. Open the link on the same device, choose a new password and sign in.

If the email does not arrive within five minutes, check your spam folder. If you use single sign-on (SSO)
through your company, reset your password with your company's identity provider instead: we do not store it.
