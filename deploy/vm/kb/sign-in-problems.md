# Problems signing in

- "Invalid credentials": check the email address for typos, then reset your password.
- "Account locked": after five failed attempts the account locks for 15 minutes. Wait, then try again.
- Single sign-on loops back to the sign-in page: clear the browser's cookies for our site, then sign in again.
- Two-factor codes are rejected: make sure your phone's clock is set automatically; codes depend on the time.
