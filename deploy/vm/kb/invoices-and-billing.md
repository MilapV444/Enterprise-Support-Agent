# Invoices and billing

Invoices are issued on the first day of each billing period and are available under Settings, then Billing,
then Invoices, as PDF downloads. Payment is taken from the card or bank account on file.

If an invoice looks wrong (a charge you do not recognise, or an amount that does not match your plan), tell
us which invoice and what looks wrong. A billing specialist reviews disputes; the assistant cannot change
invoices, refund or credit an account itself.
